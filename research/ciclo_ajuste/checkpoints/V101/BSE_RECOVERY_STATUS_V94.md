## Why this is still insufficient

The official BCRA December-2023 `Información de Entidades Financieras` confirms BSE as entity `00321` and supplies entity-level accounting aggregates, but it does not supply the Annex-Q BCRA-vs-other-FI result split required by the strict four-leg panel.

BCRA IEF source:
`https://www.bcra.gob.ar/archivos/Pdfs/PublicacionesEstadisticas/Entidades/202312e.pdf`

After the Santander counterexample, the presence of only `511108` cannot by itself be promoted into a universal `income_bcra` classification. A BSE-specific issuer presentation/crosswalk is still required.

## Recovery needed

Recover either:
1. official BSE FY-2023 financial statements with Annex Q / explicit pass counterparty opening, plus a compatible Sep bridge; or
2. an official same-entity presentation that explicitly maps the Sep/Dec raw account set to BCRA/other-FI pass legs.

Until then, BSE remains excluded from the strict numerator despite material assets.
