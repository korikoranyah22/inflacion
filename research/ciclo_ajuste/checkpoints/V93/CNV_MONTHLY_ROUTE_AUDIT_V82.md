# CNV monthly BCRA-remitted route — V82

A new official mirror/secondary public route was tested:

`https://www.argentina.gob.ar/cnv/informacion-financiera-mensual-remitida-por-bcra`

The September-2023 link resolves to:

`https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/202309cnv.pdf`

The PDF is an official monthly **Información de Entidades Financieras** compilation for entities in the public-offering regime. It exposes summarized per-entity financial evolution and indicators, but searching it did not expose the four target pass account labels/codes. BNA and Banco Ciudad were not recovered there as usable target-entity account records.

Verdict: useful corroborating route for issuer/regulatory context, **not** a substitute for the BCRA `.7z` account-level archive.
